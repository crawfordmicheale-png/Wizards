How to Play

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Space — Blink (short dash, briefly invulnerable)
  • Esc / P — pause
  • M — mute · 1–4 — pick level-up card
  • Touch: drag anywhere to move, tap the sigil to Blink

The Vigil

  • Your spells cast automatically. You only move.
  • Slain foes drop essence. Collect it to level and choose a boon.
  • Your body is large but your soul-core is tiny — enemy bullets only hit the glowing centre.
  • A Warden stirs every 2½ minutes. They drop chests.
  • Survive 15:00 and the sun burns the hollow clean.
